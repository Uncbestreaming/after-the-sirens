After the Sirens

A choice-driven survival story

How to play

Your choices shape a continuing survival story. Read the scene, check your field notes, then decide what to do.

Build your survivor

Choose a name, age, identity, work background, home or apartment, and whether you own a firearm. Pick exactly five of the 15 skills; they can reveal details and change which actions are safer.

Choose or improvise

Click an offered action to take it, or type your own action in the Improvise box. The next passage responds to what you tried and the current situation.

Watch your needs

Water and food supplies are finite. Drink and eat from the field notes to raise Thirst and Hunger. Their meters slowly fall with time and activity; the status colors shift from green to yellow, orange, and red.

Recover health

Eating or drinking restores a small amount of Health when both need meters are above 35%. Filling both Thirst and Hunger together restores Health to full. Health at zero ends the run.

Search places

Use Search room to look for supplies without advancing the current beat. Each room has a limited cache, so repeated searches eventually find nothing more there.

Manage weapons

Click a weapon in the Arsenal to equip it before choosing or typing an action. Weapons, ammunition, skills, injuries, hunger, and thirst affect dangerous encounters.

Read the situation

Work experience, selected skills, and your home type affect what you notice and which options appear. Characters can react to your actions and remember conversations.

Time and saves

The day counter tracks survival. Save now stores your game in this browser; Resume save restores it on this device. The story continues until a choice leads to a logical death.

Voice and sounds

Turn on Read story aloud to hear passages, including scene text. Soft typing and choice sounds play after you interact with the page; your device must allow audio.

Keep going

Check supplies and equipment before risky moves. There is no fixed victory ending—the story keeps creating the next beat from your decisions.
